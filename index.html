Notes on the data

Two sources

The yearly totals at the top come from the Comptroller's claims dashboard, which replaced the office's annual claims report in 2025 and is refreshed as claims are paid. It covers fiscal 2010 to the present and splits payments into tort claims (personal injury and property damage, including police action, civil rights, crashes, sidewalk falls and medical malpractice) and law claims (salary disputes, special-education tuition and services, contracts and other matters). The dashboard has no download, so the figures here were copied from its trends page on , once with the Area filter on All, once on Tort and once on Law. Tort plus law matches the total in every year, to within $1 of rounding.

Everything below the opening chart comes from the Comptroller's claim-level file on NYC Open Data (data set ex6k-ym48). That file holds tort claims only, it ends with fiscal 2023, and the city last updated it on Jan. 13, 2025. It has no law claims, no case names and no descriptions of what happened.

The two sources disagree on the same years

 

The Open Data totals match the tort payouts in the Comptroller's fiscal 2023 annual claims report ($739.6 million that year). The dashboard reports $806.8 million for the same year. The Comptroller has not published an explanation. The dashboard's figures for past years also change: its fiscal 2024 total was $1.94 billion as of April 25, 2025 and is $1.99 billion now.

What counts as a payment

The claim-level file lists settlement and judgment payments, filtered here to claim_action = SETTLED with an amount above $0. Some claims have more than one payment in different years, so the file's payments cover claim numbers. A few payments are under $1, which look like adjustments; they are left in. A settlement is not an admission of liability.

Cleaning

The file spells the same agency many ways: "Department of Transportation," "Dept OF Transportation" and "DOT - Bureau OF Traffic" are all the Transportation Department, and Health + Hospitals appears under three names. The fetch script folds each agency's spellings together, and does the same for claim types, which come in an all-caps "(PI)" form, an all-caps plain form and a mixed-case form. Boroughs are reduced to the five plus "outside the city." The original strings are kept in the downloadable file as agency_raw and type_raw.

Not here

  • Claim-level records for fiscal 2024 onward. The dashboard has a settlement detail page, but the Open Data file has not been updated since January 2025. The largest tort payment in fiscal 2024, for example, was a $171.52 million Correction Department settlement over the city's failure to promptly release people after they posted bail. It is not in the file below.
  • Claims still pending and claims closed without payment.
  • Inflation adjustment. All amounts are in the dollars of the year paid.

Updates

A monthly job reruns the fetch script against Open Data and commits any change. The dashboard totals are updated by hand. Open Data file last checked .
